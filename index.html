Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about draw verifiability, non-custodial settlements, and participant safeguards.

How is provable fairness verified on-chain? expand_more
Every draw relies on Chainlink Verifiable Random Function (VRF) paired with historical Ethereum blockhash seeds. The generated seed cannot be predicted or manipulated by node operators, protocol creators, or users. Winning ticket indexes are calculated algorithmically and verifiable on the public ledger.
When does the draw close and when are winners paid? expand_more
The entry portal locks exactly every Sunday at 23:59:59 UTC. The VRF request triggers automatically at 00:01:00 UTC Monday. Once the oracle responds (~3 to 5 minutes), the smart contract executes immediate automated USDT transfers to all 10 winners' funding wallets.
Why is there a strict 50-ticket maximum cap per wallet? expand_more
To prevent high-net-worth capital entities ("whales") from overwhelming the prize pool and driving participant odds to uncompetitive levels. This hard protocol cap ensures genuine peer distribution and community accessibility.
What happens if an unsupported token or wrong network is sent? expand_more
Only standard Tether (USDT) on TRC20, ERC20, Polygon, and Arbitrum are recognized by our escrow contracts. Unrecognized ERC20 tokens or direct non-USDT crypto sent to contract addresses cannot be automatically converted and are subject to network burn. Always verify the token symbol.
Are there any platform fees deducted from the disclosed prizes? expand_more
No. The prizes shown in the matrix (e.g. 700 USDT for 1st, 500 USDT for 2nd) are paid in full. Platform operational maintenance is sustained through protocol liquidity staking margins, not deductions from winning payouts.
